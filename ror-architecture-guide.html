Reltio-on-Reltio architecture

How Reltio uses Reltio Data Cloud to master its own customers, deployments, contracts, and product tenants. Three Cytoscape pictures from the live configuration, with a plain-language read of each.

1. What this is, in one minute

Reltio-on-Reltio (RoR) is Reltio’s production tenant of Reltio Data Cloud. It is not a SKU we sell. It is the graph account teams, Professional Services, Support, and Forge agents query when they need a trusted view of a customer.

Everyday words map to these record types:

  • Account → Organization (filter OrganizationType = Customer for paying accounts)
  • How they are deployed → Customer Deployment (the join hub)
  • What they bought → Contract (there is no separate Subscription type)
  • Instance they run → Tenant (Reltio’s record about their product instance)
  • People at Reltio → Reltian (entity type Employee)
  • People at the customer → Contact

About 40,000 organization profiles exist; on the order of 200 are paying customers.

2. Figure 1 — all entity types

This is the full L3 entity graph: 15 colored circles and the relationships between them. Sources, connectors, and capabilities are not on this picture — they are Figure 3.

How to read Figure 1

Start at the big hubs, not at the edges.

  1. Organization is the company (the account). Loops on that circle are parent/child and partner graphs (D&B Hierarchy, Organization Affiliation).
  2. Customer Deployment is the working object for a briefing. CompanyID points back to Organization. Other arrows go forward to Tenant, Contract, and Reltian.
  3. Contract is the commercial agreement. Subscription attributes live here.
  4. Tenant is the product instance they run (TenantID, cloud, region). Connector flags sit on this type — shown in Figure 3.
  5. Reltian is who at Reltio works the account. The ReportsTo loop is the manager graph.
  6. Contact is people on the customer side. Opportunity is pipeline. Engagement is Professional Services. Ticket is support. Course is learning. Location is address.

Faded / dashed circles (Individual, Household, Supplier, Campaign) are abstract types. They complete the model but are rarely queried day to day.

One path: Salesforce account → Organization. Deal closes → Contract. Go-live → Customer Deployment (with nested tenants). Support case → Ticket. Identity always starts at Organization or Customer Deployment, never at a raw tenant ID.

3. Figure 2 — commercial core (enlarged)

Most questions only need eight types. This is that subgraph, laid out left-to-right so each relationship name is readable.

How to read Figure 2

Read left to right. Each arrow is one named relationship (several names stacked means several types between the same pair).

Two walks, depending on the question:

Who is this customer? (start at Customer Deployment) Organization <-- CompanyID -- Customer Deployment --> Tenant Customer Deployment --> Contract What did they buy? (start at Organization) Organization -- OrgToContract --> Contract -- Contract To Tenant --> Tenant

Opportunity feeds Organization and Contract. Engagement is PS delivery. Contact is customer people. Reltian is the internal team.

Do not search Tenant to count Reltio’s fleet. RoR returns at most 10 rows per page and is the wrong population. Fleet inventory is the DevOps API or BigQuery tenants_report. Usage vs entitlement (CP / RSU / API) is BigQuery EDW, not RoR MCP.

4. Figure 3 — sources and connectors

This picture is the integration layer around Organization, Customer Deployment, and Tenant. It answers “what feeds RoR, and what can that Tenant do?”

How to read Figure 3

  • Green dashed circles — configured sources (Salesforce, Zendesk, Gainsight, Marketo, D&B, Okta, Skilljar, BigQuery, and others). These are systems that send data into RoR.
  • Grey boxes — platform capabilities (RDM, Match IQ, Cleanse, Workflow, Queues, Analytics, image hosting, auth). Match and cleanse collapse duplicates into one best-version profile.
  • Blue diamonds — logical graphs (D&B Hierarchy, Organization Hierarchy, ReportsTo Graph).
  • Hexagon — rule-based householding (Contact grouped into Household).

Connector flags (Salesforce, BigQuery, Reltio Integration Hub, and the rest) sit on Tenant. They are listed in the table below — they are not drawn as orange boxes on this picture.
